# Game Night dashboard artwork

Created with the built-in image-generation tool and optimized to WebP for mobile delivery. The supplied dashboard image guided the layout, colors and 3D visual style. Text, buttons, navigation and statistics remain live HTML controls.

Final assets are in `public/game-night/`: `hero.webp`, `uno.webp`, `likely.webp`, `spades.webp`, `dominoes.webp`, `ludo.webp`, and `trivia.webp`.

## Final prompts

- **Hero:** Luxurious burgundy velvet game-night scene, gold-rimmed round velvet tray on the right, colorful UNO-style cards, two glossy hot-pink dice and ivory dominoes. Left half stays empty dark wine velvet for the website heading. Warm studio light, deep wine, raspberry, blush, cream and gold. No buttons, interface or captions. Final precise edit: keep the composition unchanged and add yellow UNO with black shadow to the red front card, white 7 to yellow, white +2 to green and a reverse symbol to blue.
- **UNO:** Isolated polished 3D fan of chunky rounded cards: green +2, yellow 7 and a red UNO front card. Cream edges, soft shadows, magenta accent rays and transparent background.
- **Who’s Most Likely To:** Isolated polished 3D pink and plum friend-avatar tokens, the front token wearing a small gold crown, two ivory voting cards with magenta question marks and a pink speech bubble with cartoon eyes. Raspberry, blush and gold, soft shadows and transparency.
- **Spades:** Isolated polished 3D ivory ace of spades, king of hearts and queen of hearts, fanned behind a glossy black spade with a gold rim and a gold sparkle. Cream edges, soft shadows and transparent background.
- **Dominoes:** Two chunky ivory domino tiles with glossy black pips, tilted and overlapping, a tiny gold sparkle and magenta accent rays. Premium playful 3D rendering on transparency.
- **Ludo:** A small Ludo board in perspective, rounded wooden border, glossy red, blue, green and yellow pawns and a white die. Cute dimensional product rendering, soft shadows, magenta accent rays and transparent background.
- **Trivia:** A glossy raspberry quiz buzzer with gold rim, two ivory question-mark cards behind it, a gold sparkle and two blush speech bubbles. Rounded premium 3D rendering, transparent background and no UI labels.

- **Velvet table** (`public/game-night/velvet-table-pink.webp`): Built-in image generation, then precise color edit: horizontal oval tabletop with realistic plush velvet nap, raised padded stitched rim, transparent surroundings. Change only its burgundy color to slightly lighter, pinker raspberry rose velvet; preserve silhouette, fibers, lighting and transparency. No objects or text.
- **Card back** (`public/game-night/card-back.webp`): Built-in image generation: straight upright rounded white-rim card, saturated raspberry pink velvet-like gradient, inner rose border, central white sparkle in circle and UNO, subtle paper curvature and edge thickness, transparent background.
